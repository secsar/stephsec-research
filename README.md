# StephSec Research
### Cybersecurity assurance • Cyberwarfare • Cybercrime • Intelligence

The public research workspace of **Stéphane Ikoué**.

This repository organizes research questions, study designs, source evaluation, and public research notes at the intersection of cybersecurity and national security.

[Professional website](https://stephsec.com) · [Research agenda](research/agenda.md) · [Research method](docs/research-method.md)

## Research interests

| Area | Research direction |
| --- | --- |
| Cybersecurity assurance | Evidence supporting security claims and confidence in system behavior |
| Cyberwarfare | Cyber operations, strategic effects, attribution uncertainty, and civilian consequences |
| Cyber military selective service | Comparative questions about cyber workforce mobilization, readiness, governance, and safeguards |
| Cybercrime and international cooperation | Coordination, evidence handling, and institutional barriers across jurisdictions |
| Botnets in African contexts | Measurement quality, infrastructure abuse, regional context, and defensive coordination |
| Intelligence and national security | Source reliability, competing explanations, and communication of uncertainty |

These are areas of inquiry. Their inclusion does not imply endorsement of a policy, attribution of an operation, or a completed empirical finding.

## Status

The initial release contains an agenda, a proposed study protocol, and reusable research materials. It contains **no completed literature review, empirical dataset, peer-reviewed publication, or verified research result**.

Materials will identify their status and limitations individually. A research question is not a conclusion; a study protocol is not a completed study.
