## Source selection and limits

The landscape review used primary project READMEs, official institutional resource pages, and one explicitly labeled preprint. The project descriptions establish advertised scope; no production deployments or security claims of those tools were independently audited.

The INTERPOL source is an official assessment announcement based on a survey, not an independent measurement of all African networks. The ICRC source informs a civilian-protection research lens, not legal advice. The NICE and ODNI sources inform terminology and analytical discipline, not certification or affiliation.

Every platform capability that has not been implemented is identified as proposed. A literature source can motivate a hypothesis but cannot supply results for an experiment that has not run.
